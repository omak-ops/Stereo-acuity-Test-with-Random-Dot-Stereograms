Independent sampling

Random means room for clusters. Every dot center is sampled independently from the canvas pixels. Dots can overlap, and dots at the edges are cropped. Coverage is the dots' total area as a share of the image, before overlaps are taken into account. A seed gives the same dot positions whatever the colors.

Made for exploring random dots. Everything runs in your browser, even offline. Settings are saved in the page link, so “Copy link” shares the exact pattern.
